Got Questions?

Rooftop Solar & PM Surya Ghar FAQs

Clear answers regarding cost, subsidies, net metering, and installation in Karnataka.

Is Sri Energy an authorized partner for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy? ▾
Yes. Sri Energy designs and installs rooftop solar systems that comply strictly with Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) guidelines and qualify for the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana in Karnataka. We handle DISCOM approvals (CHESCOM / BESCOM) and help you claim up to ₹78,000 directly.
How much does a rooftop solar plant cost in Mysore? ▾
Residential solar systems in Mysore typically cost ₹45,000 to ₹65,000 per kW before subsidy. For a standard 3 kW system (approx. ₹1.85 Lakhs total), after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar central subsidy, your net out-of-pocket investment is only around ₹1.05 to ₹1.15 Lakhs, which pays for itself in under 3.5 to 4 years.
Do you install solar in Coorg and rural agricultural areas? ▾
Yes! We have dedicated teams serving Kodagu (Coorg), Mandya, Hassan, and Chamarajanagar. We specialize in hybrid solar systems with lithium batteries for estates experiencing frequent power cuts and solar pumpsets for coffee plantations and agricultural farms.
On-grid, off-grid or hybrid — which system should I choose? ▾
• On-Grid is the most economical if your area has stable power and you want maximum electricity bill reduction with net metering.
• Hybrid is ideal if you experience frequent power cuts and require battery backup for lights, fans, fridge, and WiFi.
• Off-Grid suits remote farmhouses without any electricity grid connection.
