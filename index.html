Common questions

Rooftop solar FAQ — Mysore & Coorg

Is Sri Energy eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy? ▼

Yes. We design and install rooftop solar systems that qualify for the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana in Karnataka. We handle paperwork and DISCOM approvals for homes in Mysore, Coorg and nearby districts.

How much does rooftop solar cost in Mysore? ▼

Typical residential systems range from about ₹45,000–₹70,000 per kW before subsidy, depending on panel brand, inverter and roof type. After PM Surya Ghar subsidy the net cost is much lower. Request a free site survey for an exact quote.

Do you install solar in Coorg and rural areas? ▼

Yes. We serve Mysuru, Kodagu (Coorg), Mandya, Hassan and surrounding areas — including solar pumpsets for farmers and hybrid systems for homes with unreliable grid power.

On-grid, off-grid or hybrid — which should I choose? ▼

On-grid suits stable electricity and net metering. Hybrid with lithium battery is best for power cuts. Off-grid fits remote locations. We recommend the right type after a free load and site assessment. Read the full comparison →
