Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

1. Overview & Commitment

Sri Energy ("we", "our", or "us"), operating out of Mysuru, Karnataka, is committed to safeguarding your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and protect information collected through our website (srienergy.in), phone inquiries, WhatsApp consultations, and physical site inspections.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect personal information necessary to calculate your solar savings, prepare customized EPC proposals, and process PM Surya Ghar subsidy paperwork:

  • Contact Information: Full name, mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact number, and email address.
  • Installation Site Details: Property address, city/taluk (e.g. Mysuru, Madikeri, Mandya, Hassan), pincode, and type of roof (RCC terrace, sheet, or tiled).
  • Utility Bill Information: Average monthly electricity bill amount, current tariff category, and Consumer Identification Number (CA / RR Number) provided voluntarily for net-metering feasibility assessment.
  • Technical Data: Anonymized website analytics (browser type, device viewport, referring source) to optimize site performance and accessibility.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data strictly under the principles of purpose limitation and explicit customer consent:

  • Generating accurate solar system sizing estimates and subsidy breakdowns.
  • Contacting you via phone call or WhatsApp to confirm site survey availability.
  • Facilitating statutory submissions to the National Portal for PM Surya Ghar and electricity distribution licensees (CHESCOM / BESCOM) for bi-directional net metering approval.
  • Providing post-installation system monitoring, maintenance alerts, and warranty records.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

Sri Energy does NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data with third-party advertisers or lead-generation brokers.

Data is shared strictly with authorized entities only when required to fulfill your solar installation:

  • Government & Utility Portals: MNRE National Portal for PM Surya Ghar subsidy verification and CHESCOM / BESCOM for grid net-meter testing.
  • Hardware OEMs: Loom Solar, RenewSys, and inverter/battery manufacturers solely for issuing official equipment warranty certificates.

5. Data Retention & Security Controls

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Customer records are retained for the duration of the 25-year equipment warranty lifecycle or until you request deletion.

6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal, you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of personal data held about you and how it has been processed.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate contact or site records.
  • Withdraw your consent to receive promotional or quotation follow-ups at any time.
  • Nominate another individual in the event of death or incapacity.
