Solar Pumpsets for Farmers in Coorg & Mysore — Save on Irrigation Power

Irrigation is one of the biggest energy costs for farmers in Coorg, Mysore and surrounding districts. A solar pumpset replaces diesel or expensive grid power with free sunlight during the day.

Why farmers switch to solar pumps

  • Near-zero daytime running cost after installation
  • No diesel logistics or engine maintenance
  • Works in remote fields without reliable grid
  • Long life with minimal upkeep

Sizing basics

Pump capacity depends on water depth, daily water requirement and pipe length. Sri Energy helps size the panel array and pump so you get the flow you need without overspending.

Support for agriculture

State and central programmes sometimes support solar pumps for agriculture. We can guide you on current schemes applicable in Karnataka when you request a site assessment.
