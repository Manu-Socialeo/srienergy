Rooftop Solar Cost in Mysore 2026 — Price per kW After Subsidy

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

In Mysore, a residential rooftop solar system typically costs in a range that depends on panel quality, inverter type, structure and labour. After PM Surya Ghar subsidy, the effective cost for a 1–3 kW system drops significantly.

What drives the price?

  • Panel wattage and brand (e.g. Paradea, RenewSys, Hypersol)
  • Inverter type: on-grid vs hybrid
  • Mounting structure (RCC roof vs metal shed)
  • Wiring, earthing, net meter and permissions
  • Whether lithium battery backup is added

After subsidy — effective cost

For systems up to 3 kW, central subsidy under PM Surya Ghar can reduce your net investment by tens of thousands of rupees. Exact numbers change with policy updates — always get a site-specific quote and check the official portal.

Payback in Mysore

With good generation (Mysore has strong solar resource) and net metering, many households see payback in about 3–6 years depending on tariff and self-consumption. After that, the system continues to cut bills for 20+ years.
