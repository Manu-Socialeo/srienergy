PM Surya Ghar Subsidy 2026 — Rates, Eligibility & How to Apply in Karnataka

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Sri Energy, Mysuru

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the Government of India’s residential rooftop solar subsidy scheme. If you live in Mysore, Coorg or anywhere in Karnataka, you can get a central financial subsidy that significantly reduces the cost of installing a grid-connected rooftop solar system.

How much subsidy do you get?

Under the current structure of the scheme (confirm latest figures on the official portal before applying):

  • First 2 kW: higher per-kW subsidy (commonly cited as ₹30,000/kW)
  • Additional capacity up to 3 kW: lower per-kW rate (commonly cited as ₹18,000/kW)
  • Maximum central subsidy for a typical 3 kW residential system is often around ₹78,000

Subsidy is released after installation and inspection as per DISCOM / portal process. Always verify current rates on pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Who is eligible?

  • Individual residential consumers with an electricity connection in their name
  • Suitable rooftop or elevated structure that can hold the solar panels safely
  • Grid-connected (on-grid) rooftop system installed by a registered vendor
  • RWAs and housing societies may have separate provisions under the same programme

Documents you typically need

  • Latest electricity bill
  • Aadhaar and identity proof
  • Proof of ownership / occupancy of the premises
  • Bank account details for subsidy credit
  • Photos of the site (as required by the portal)

Frequently asked questions

Is the subsidy available for hybrid or off-grid systems?

PM Surya Ghar is primarily for grid-connected residential rooftop systems. Hybrid systems that export to the grid may qualify depending on configuration and DISCOM rules; pure off-grid systems generally do not get this particular subsidy.

How long does subsidy disbursement take?

After installation, inspection and successful application processing, subsidy is typically credited to your bank account as per the timelines published on the national portal and by your DISCOM. Delays can occur if documents are incomplete.

Can Sri Energy apply on my behalf?

We assist with technical documents, installation reports and process guidance. Final application and Aadhaar-based authentication are usually done by the consumer on the official portal.
