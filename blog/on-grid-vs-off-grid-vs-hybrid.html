On-Grid vs Off-Grid vs Hybrid Solar — Which Is Best for Your Home?

Choosing the right solar system type is the most important decision after deciding to go solar. Here’s a clear comparison for homes in Mysore and Coorg.

On-Grid (Grid-Tied)

Connects to the electricity grid. Excess power is exported under net metering. Lowest cost, no batteries. Shuts down during power cuts (anti-islanding safety). Best if your grid is fairly reliable and you want maximum subsidy eligibility under PM Surya Ghar.

Off-Grid

Fully independent with battery bank. Works without any grid connection. Higher cost due to batteries. Ideal for remote farms or sites with no grid. Not the typical choice for urban Mysore homes.

Hybrid

Grid connection + battery backup. You get net metering benefits and power during outages. Higher upfront cost than pure on-grid, but better resilience. Popular where load-shedding is a concern.

Quick recommendation

  • Stable grid, want lowest cost + subsidy: On-Grid
  • Frequent outages, want backup: Hybrid
  • No grid or full independence: Off-Grid
